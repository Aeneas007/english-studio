# KJV text source

Source: https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/
Download: https://ebible.org/Scriptures/eng-kjv_html.zip
Source rights notice: https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/copyright.htm

King James Version, standardized 1769 text. The source includes Apocrypha; this app selects the conventional 66-book Protestant canon only. 1,189 chapters and 31,102 numbered verses were validated after conversion. Inline source footnotes and page navigation are excluded; verse text is retained. No Chinese translation is included.

eBible.org identifies this text as public domain outside the United Kingdom, where the source explains the Crown printing rights. Source page dated 2026-09-26; HTML generated 2026-10-13. Retrieved 2026-10-14.

The app loads each book on demand. Books opened online are cached by its service worker and can subsequently be read offline on that browser. Not-yet-opened books require a network connection. Book names in Chinese are navigation labels only.
